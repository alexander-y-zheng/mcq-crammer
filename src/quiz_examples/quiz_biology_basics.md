### 1. What is the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms?
- [ ] Tissue
- [x] Cell
- [ ] Organ
- [ ] Organelle
> Explanation: The cell is considered the fundamental building block of life, capable of carrying out all essential life processes.

### 2. Which organelle is known as the "powerhouse of the cell"?
- [ ] Nucleus
- [x] Mitochondria
- [ ] Ribosome
- [ ] Golgi apparatus
> Explanation: Mitochondria generate most of the cell's ATP through cellular respiration, earning them the nickname "powerhouse of the cell."

### 3. What molecule carries genetic information in most living organisms?
- [ ] RNA
- [x] DNA
- [ ] ATP
- [ ] Protein
> Explanation: DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) stores the genetic instructions used in the growth, development, and functioning of organisms.

### 4. What is the process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy?
- [ ] Respiration
- [x] Photosynthesis
- [ ] Fermentation
- [ ] Transpiration
> Explanation: Photosynthesis allows plants to convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen.

### 5. Which of the following is NOT one of the four main types of biological macromolecules?
- [ ] Carbohydrates
- [ ] Proteins
- [x] Minerals
- [ ] Lipids
> Explanation: The four major classes of biological macromolecules are carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids; minerals are inorganic and not macromolecules.

### 6. What is the process of cell division that produces two genetically identical daughter cells called?
- [ ] Meiosis
- [x] Mitosis
- [ ] Fertilization
- [ ] Osmosis
> Explanation: Mitosis is the type of cell division that results in two daughter cells, each with the same genetic material as the parent cell.

### 7. Which part of the cell controls what enters and exits the cell?
- [ ] Nucleus
- [ ] Cytoplasm
- [x] Cell membrane
- [ ] Cell wall
> Explanation: The cell membrane is selectively permeable, regulating the movement of substances into and out of the cell.

### 8. What is the term for an organism's complete set of genetic material?
- [ ] Phenotype
- [x] Genome
- [ ] Genotype
- [ ] Chromosome
> Explanation: The genome refers to the entirety of an organism's hereditary information encoded in its DNA.

### 9. Which biomolecule serves as the primary source of quick energy for cells?
- [x] Carbohydrates
- [ ] Proteins
- [ ] Lipids
- [ ] Nucleic acids
> Explanation: Carbohydrates, particularly glucose, are broken down quickly to provide readily available energy for cellular processes.

### 10. What is the term for organisms that produce their own food using light or chemical energy?
- [ ] Heterotrophs
- [x] Autotrophs
- [ ] Decomposers
- [ ] Parasites
> Explanation: Autotrophs, such as plants and some bacteria, synthesize their own organic compounds from inorganic sources using light or chemical energy.
