### 1. Which ancient civilization built the pyramids at Giza?
- [ ] Mesopotamians
- [x] Ancient Egyptians
- [ ] Romans
- [ ] Aztecs
> Explanation: The pyramids at Giza were constructed by the ancient Egyptians, primarily as tombs for pharaohs.

### 2. What event marked the beginning of World War I?
- [ ] The bombing of Pearl Harbor
- [x] The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand
- [ ] The invasion of Poland
- [ ] The signing of the Treaty of Versailles
> Explanation: The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary in 1914 triggered a chain of alliances that led to World War I.

### 3. Who was the first President of the United States?
- [ ] Thomas Jefferson
- [x] George Washington
- [ ] John Adams
- [ ] Abraham Lincoln
> Explanation: George Washington served as the first President of the United States from 1789 to 1797.

### 4. Which empire was ruled by Genghis Khan?
- [ ] Ottoman Empire
- [x] Mongol Empire
- [ ] Roman Empire
- [ ] Persian Empire
> Explanation: Genghis Khan founded and led the Mongol Empire, which became the largest contiguous land empire in history.

### 5. What was the Renaissance primarily known for?
- [ ] A period of military conquest
- [x] A revival of art, culture, and learning in Europe
- [ ] The industrialization of Europe
- [ ] The spread of the Black Death
> Explanation: The Renaissance was a cultural movement spanning roughly the 14th to 17th centuries, marked by renewed interest in classical art, science, and philosophy.

### 6. Which wall symbolized the division between East and West during the Cold War?
- [ ] The Great Wall of China
- [x] The Berlin Wall
- [ ] Hadrian's Wall
- [ ] The Western Wall
> Explanation: The Berlin Wall divided East and West Berlin from 1961 to 1989 and became a powerful symbol of Cold War division.

### 7. Who wrote the "Declaration of Independence" of the United States?
- [ ] Benjamin Franklin
- [x] Thomas Jefferson
- [ ] Alexander Hamilton
- [ ] James Madison
> Explanation: Thomas Jefferson was the principal author of the Declaration of Independence, adopted in 1776.

### 8. Which ancient wonder of the world was located in Alexandria, Egypt?
- [ ] The Colossus of Rhodes
- [x] The Lighthouse of Alexandria
- [ ] The Hanging Gardens
- [ ] The Statue of Zeus
> Explanation: The Lighthouse of Alexandria (Pharos) was one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, guiding ships into the city's harbor.

### 9. What was the main cause of the French Revolution?
- [ ] A foreign invasion
- [x] Widespread social inequality and financial crisis
- [ ] A natural disaster
- [ ] Religious conflict alone
> Explanation: The French Revolution was driven largely by deep social inequality, economic hardship, and dissatisfaction with the monarchy.

### 10. Which empire was known for constructing an extensive network of roads and aqueducts across three continents?
- [ ] Han Dynasty
- [x] Roman Empire
- [ ] Inca Empire
- [ ] Byzantine Empire
> Explanation: The Roman Empire built an extensive infrastructure of roads and aqueducts spanning Europe, North Africa, and parts of Asia to support trade, military movement, and water supply.
