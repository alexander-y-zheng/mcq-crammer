### 1. What is the chemical symbol for gold?
- [ ] Ag
- [x] Au
- [ ] Gd
- [ ] Go
> Explanation: The symbol "Au" comes from the Latin word for gold, "aurum."

### 2. What is the pH of a neutral solution at 25°C?
- [ ] 0
- [x] 7
- [ ] 14
- [ ] 10
> Explanation: A pH of 7 indicates a neutral solution, neither acidic nor basic, at standard temperature.

### 3. What subatomic particle has a negative charge?
- [ ] Proton
- [x] Electron
- [ ] Neutron
- [ ] Positron
> Explanation: Electrons carry a negative electric charge and orbit the nucleus of an atom.

### 4. What type of bond involves the sharing of electron pairs between atoms?
- [ ] Ionic bond
- [x] Covalent bond
- [ ] Metallic bond
- [ ] Hydrogen bond
> Explanation: Covalent bonds form when two atoms share one or more pairs of electrons.

### 5. What is the chemical formula for water?
- [ ] CO2
- [x] H2O
- [ ] O2
- [ ] H2O2
> Explanation: Water consists of two hydrogen atoms covalently bonded to one oxygen atom, giving it the formula H2O.

### 6. Which gas do plants absorb from the atmosphere for photosynthesis?
- [ ] Oxygen
- [x] Carbon dioxide
- [ ] Nitrogen
- [ ] Hydrogen
> Explanation: Plants take in carbon dioxide from the air and use it, along with water and light, to produce glucose during photosynthesis.

### 7. What is the term for a substance that speeds up a chemical reaction without being consumed?
- [ ] Reactant
- [x] Catalyst
- [ ] Solvent
- [ ] Isotope
> Explanation: A catalyst lowers the activation energy of a reaction, speeding it up, without itself being permanently changed or consumed.

### 8. What is the atomic number of an element determined by?
- [ ] Number of neutrons
- [x] Number of protons
- [ ] Number of electrons in the outer shell
- [ ] Atomic mass
> Explanation: The atomic number is defined by the number of protons in the nucleus of an atom, which determines the element's identity.

### 9. Which state of matter has a definite volume but no definite shape?
- [ ] Solid
- [x] Liquid
- [ ] Gas
- [ ] Plasma
> Explanation: Liquids maintain a constant volume but take the shape of their container, unlike solids (fixed shape) or gases (no fixed volume).

### 10. What is the process called when a substance changes directly from a solid to a gas without passing through the liquid phase?
- [ ] Evaporation
- [x] Sublimation
- [ ] Condensation
- [ ] Deposition
> Explanation: Sublimation occurs when a solid transitions directly into a gas, as seen with dry ice (solid carbon dioxide).
