### 1. What is the name of the galaxy that contains our solar system?
- [ ] Andromeda
- [x] Milky Way
- [ ] Triangulum
- [ ] Whirlpool
> Explanation: Our solar system resides within the Milky Way galaxy, a barred spiral galaxy containing hundreds of billions of stars.

### 2. Which planet is known as the "Red Planet"?
- [ ] Jupiter
- [x] Mars
- [ ] Venus
- [ ] Mercury
> Explanation: Mars appears reddish due to iron oxide (rust) covering much of its surface.

### 3. What is a light-year a unit of?
- [ ] Time
- [x] Distance
- [ ] Brightness
- [ ] Mass
> Explanation: A light-year measures the distance light travels in one year, roughly 9.46 trillion kilometers, not a unit of time.

### 4. Which force is primarily responsible for holding planets in orbit around the Sun?
- [ ] Magnetism
- [x] Gravity
- [ ] Friction
- [ ] Nuclear force
> Explanation: Gravity is the attractive force between masses that keeps planets in their orbital paths around the Sun.

### 5. What is the closest star to Earth (other than the Sun)?
- [ ] Sirius
- [x] Proxima Centauri
- [ ] Betelgeuse
- [ ] Polaris
> Explanation: Proxima Centauri, part of the Alpha Centauri star system, is the nearest known star to our Sun.

### 6. What type of celestial object is formed when a massive star collapses under its own gravity?
- [ ] White dwarf
- [x] Black hole
- [ ] Comet
- [ ] Asteroid
> Explanation: When a sufficiently massive star exhausts its fuel and collapses, it can form a black hole, an object with gravity so strong not even light escapes.

### 7. What causes the phases of the Moon?
- [ ] Earth's shadow covering the Moon
- [x] Changing angles of sunlight reflecting off the Moon as it orbits Earth
- [ ] The Moon changing shape
- [ ] Clouds blocking the Moon
> Explanation: Lunar phases result from the changing relative positions of the Earth, Moon, and Sun, altering how much of the sunlit Moon is visible from Earth.

### 8. Which planet has the most extensive ring system in our solar system?
- [ ] Uranus
- [x] Saturn
- [ ] Neptune
- [ ] Jupiter
> Explanation: Saturn's rings, composed mainly of ice and rock particles, are the largest and most visible in the solar system.

### 9. What is the term for a star's explosive death?
- [ ] Eclipse
- [x] Supernova
- [ ] Nebula
- [ ] Solstice
> Explanation: A supernova is a powerful stellar explosion that occurs at the end of certain stars' life cycles.

### 10. What is the name of the theory describing the origin and expansion of the universe?
- [ ] Steady State Theory
- [x] Big Bang Theory
- [ ] String Theory
- [ ] Multiverse Theory
> Explanation: The Big Bang Theory describes how the universe originated from an extremely hot, dense state and has been expanding ever since.
